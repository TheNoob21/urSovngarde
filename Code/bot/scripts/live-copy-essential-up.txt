# A copy of an essential NPC that went into its own bleedout here must get up once its owner's NPC is up, even when the
# owner's "bleedOutStop" never takes on it.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-copy-essential-up
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py --expect-fail live-copy-essential-up     on a build without the fix: must be RED
#   set LIVE_CHECK_DEVBENCH=http://127.0.0.1:8922 first for the flat rig (Tools\Flat\flat-rig.ps1 up)
#   live-copy-essential-up-dead is its negative control: the same, with the owner's NPC dead instead of up.
#
# Play, 2026-11-06 18:00-18:15 (VR_TODO "An essential NPC copy stays down"): Lydia's copy took her owner's low health,
# went into its own bleedout (life state 8, knock state 5), and her owner's bleedOutStop was refused as an action and as
# a graph event. Copies finish no bleedout by themselves, and every get-up repair skipped a copy that bleeds out, so she
# lay there for good while her owner's Lydia fought on.
# As live-copy-bleedout, the game places a Lydia (base A2C8E, made essential), knocks her down and stands her up, and sets
# her on the player so that her owner sends actions with her up, while the bot records it. The bot's own Lydia plays the
# recording without its bleedOutStop: the deterministic form of "refused". Then the owner's health comes back.
#
# A run counts only when (REQUIRE): the recording holds the owner's get-up, the owner's NPC was down, the copy is the
# owner's kind, it lay down and bled out here. It is then judged, 5 s after the recording's last action: on its feet,
# no longer bleeding out, alive, and it got up where the owner's NPC did (from where each stood).
log live copy essential up
waitfor connected 45
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 5

log DO god on
log -- a Lydia ahead of the player, essential so she goes down instead of dying (the bot's below shares the base)
log DO papyrus ActorBase SetEssential A2C8E b:true
log DO place A2C8E essential
wait 8

log -- the game knocks her down, stands her up again and sets her on the player while the bot records it
log DO record original placed 24
log DO sleep 3
log DO papyrus Actor DamageActorValue placed s:Health f:100000
log DO sleep 9
log DO papyrus Actor RestoreActorValue placed s:Health f:100000
log DO sleep 3
log DO combat on
log DO papyrus Actor StartCombat placed 14
capture newest 22
log DO combat off
log DO papyrus Actor IsBleedingOut placed
log REQUIRE bot logged Captured action names of \w+: .*\bbleedOutStop\b
log REQUIRE logged DownedTravel: own NPC \w+ moved \d+ units in \d+ ms while down \(life state 7

log -- the bot brings its own Lydia and replays the recording on her, without its bleedOutStop
npc temp A2C8E
waitfor owned == 1 20
wait 6
# The sync health floor keeps a copy alive at 1. Prove its native bleedout immediately after damage.
# A later owner action can erase that life word while its graph remains down.
log DO record copy npc 40
npclife alive 1
wait 1
log DO papyrus Actor DamageActorValue npc s:Health f:100000
wait 1
log REQUIRE npc bleedout true
wait 3
# The client poses no body out of view, and the bot stands 200 units east of the player, behind the headset on the rig's save.
log DO look at npc 500 hold 36000
wait 1
log REQUIRE view npc
# "DO watch" holds the driver for its whole time, so the recording and the bleedout check come before it.
# Down in the recording by then: the copy bleeds out here.
log DO sleep 6
log DO watch npc 26
replay npc 1 skip bleedOutStop
# The owner's health back, as its value tick would send it once she is up.
npclife alive 100000
wait 5
log SHOT after of npc
log REQUIRE absent Base form differs
# A bleedout kneels as well as lies: require the body to stay down for a while, apart from the check above.
log REQUIRE npc was down
log CHECK npc standing
log CHECK npc bleedout false
log CHECK npc alive
log CHECK place copy like original 64
log CHECK game alive
log DO papyrus ActorBase SetEssential A2C8E b:false
log DO god off
wait 3
log done
stop
