# Another player's killing blow on a protected or essential NPC this game runs only puts it down: the game lets only its own player
# kill a protected NPC, and nobody an essential one. It never dies here from that blow, and no death goes out.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-owner-blow-protected
#
# Here this game places a Lydia, protected, and the bot hits her for more than her health; then an essential one, the same.
log live owner blow protected
waitfor connected 45
collect death
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 5
stay

log -- a protected Lydia this game owns, beside the player
log DO papyrus ActorBase SetEssential A2C8E b:false
log DO papyrus ActorBase SetProtected A2C8E b:true
log DO place A2C8E
wait 8
log DO logmark
hit base:A2C8E -2000
wait 4
log CHECK logged Death sync: player \d+'s blow of 2000 on this game's NPC
log CHECK absent Death sync: health broadcast killed actor
log CHECK absent Death sync: local actor \w+ is now dead
log DO papyrus Actor IsDead placed
log DO papyrus Actor IsBleedingOut placed

log -- an essential Lydia, the same blow
log DO papyrus ActorBase SetProtected A2C8E b:false
log DO papyrus ActorBase SetEssential A2C8E b:true
log DO place A2C8E
wait 8
log DO logmark
hit base:A2C8E -2000
wait 4
log CHECK logged Death sync: player \d+'s blow of 2000 on this game's NPC
log CHECK absent Death sync: health broadcast killed actor
log CHECK absent Death sync: local actor \w+ is now dead
log DO papyrus Actor IsDead placed
log DO papyrus Actor IsBleedingOut placed
log DO papyrus ActorBase SetEssential A2C8E b:false
log CHECK game alive
wait 2
log done
stop
