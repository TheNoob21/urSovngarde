# The negative control of live-copy-essential-up: a copy whose owner's NPC died must not be stood up, even when the
# owner's actions that arrive after its death say it was up.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-copy-essential-up-dead
#   set LIVE_CHECK_DEVBENCH=http://127.0.0.1:8922 first for the flat rig (Tools\Flat\flat-rig.ps1 up)
#
# The same recording as live-copy-essential-up, but the owner says its Lydia is dead while her copy bleeds out here,
# before the replay: the actions that follow, recorded with her up, arrive at a copy whose owner says dead.
# The get-up that takes a bleeding-out copy out when its owner is up must never fire then. An essential copy dies on
# its owner's word (live-protected-copy-dies), and a dead copy plays nothing more.
# It passes on a build without the fix too: it guards the fix, it does not test for a bug.
log live copy essential up dead
waitfor connected 45
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 5

log DO god on
log -- a Lydia ahead of the player, essential so she goes down instead of dying (the bot's below shares the base)
log DO papyrus ActorBase SetEssential A2C8E b:true
log DO place A2C8E essential
wait 8

log -- the game knocks her down, stands her up again and sets her on the player while the bot records it
log DO sleep 3
log DO papyrus Actor DamageActorValue placed s:Health f:100000
log DO sleep 9
log DO papyrus Actor RestoreActorValue placed s:Health f:100000
log DO sleep 3
log DO combat on
log DO papyrus Actor StartCombat placed 14
capture newest 22
log DO combat off
log REQUIRE bot logged Captured action names of \w+: .*\bbleedOutStop\b

log -- the bot brings its own Lydia; she goes down here, then her owner says she died, then the recording plays
npc temp A2C8E
waitfor owned == 1 20
wait 6
npclife alive 1
wait 1
log DO papyrus Actor DamageActorValue npc s:Health f:100000
wait 1
log REQUIRE npc bleedout true
wait 3
log DO look at npc 500 hold 36000
wait 1
log REQUIRE view npc
npclife dead 0
wait 3
log DO logmark
replay npc 1 skip bleedOutStop
wait 5
log SHOT after of npc
log REQUIRE absent Base form differs
log CHECK npc dead
log CHECK absent KnockDown: NPC copy \w+ .* taken out of its bleedout
log CHECK game alive
log DO papyrus ActorBase SetEssential A2C8E b:false
log DO god off
wait 3
log done
stop
