# This game's player gives a word to another player's follower, and trades with her copy here (driven from outside: the box needs DevBench's menu calls).
# The bot's player has Lydia (A2C94) as a follower; this game shows her on a copy. The bot answers every word as done, as her game would.
# The outside driver activates the copy, presses Wait here, then Follow her player, then Trade, holds the trade menu 5 s and closes it.
log live follower word asker
waitfor connected 45
collect ownership party
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
ownership accept
wait 5

log DO god on
log DO papyrus ObjectReference Enable A2C94 b:false
log DO papyrus ObjectReference MoveTo A2C94 14 f:150 f:0 f:0 b:false
log DO papyrus Actor SetActorValue A2C94 s:WaitingForPlayer f:1
log DO papyrus Actor SetPlayerTeammate A2C94 b:false b:false
waitfor known ref:A2C94 40
wait 5
claim ref:A2C94 follower
stay
waitfor ownedby ref:A2C94 me 20
expect ownedby ref:A2C94 me
wait 4
log READY for the driver
wait 90
log -- after the words and the trade, she is still the bot's player's follower
expect ownedby ref:A2C94 me
wait 2
log done
stop
