# An NPC turns on another player whose hostile spell does no damage, as it does for a hit; a frenzy is left to the game.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-hostile-spell
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py --expect-fail live-hostile-spell     on a build without the fix: must be RED
#
# This game places an Anoriath of its own, who is no enemy of anyone. The bot's character casts spells on him, as the
# other player's game sends them: an AddTarget, and no health change, because none of these does damage.
# Before, the effect was applied here and the caster stayed out of his fight: he could be paralysed again and again.
# Skyrim.esm ids, read from the plugin: Heal Other 12FD2 (RestoreHealthFFActor 1CEA7, not hostile), Paralyze 5AD5F
# (ParalysisFFAimed 1EA6E, hostile), Fury 4DEEB (InfluenceAggUpFFAimed 4DEE6, hostile, frenzy).
#
# Pass rules, in order:
# 1. REQUIRE: the placed Anoriath is here and fights nobody, so what follows starts from peace.
# 2. A heal (not hostile) starts no fight.
# 3. A frenzy starts no fight with the caster: it decides whom he fights itself.
# 4. Paralysis (hostile, no damage) puts the bot's character into his fight: the client says so and he fights the copy.
log live hostile spell
waitfor connected 45
collect spawn ownership
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 5
stay
ownership refuse
log DO logmark
log DO god on
log DO combat on
wait 2

log -- this game places an Anoriath
log DO place 13B97
wait 6
log REQUIRE placed fights none

log -- a heal from the bot: no fight
effect base:13B97 12FD2 1CEA7 20
wait 3
log CHECK absent failed to retrieve (spell|effect)
log CHECK absent Combat: actor FF[0-9A-F]+ now also fights player
log CHECK placed fights none

log -- a frenzy from the bot: left to the game, no fight with the bot's character
effect base:13B97 4DEEB 4DEE6 50
wait 3
log CHECK absent Combat: actor FF[0-9A-F]+ now also fights player
log DO papyrus ObjectReference Disable placed b:false
wait 2

log -- a fresh Anoriath, since the frenzy lasts: paralysis from the bot, and he fights the bot's character
log DO place 13B97
wait 6
log REQUIRE placed fights none
log DO logmark
effect base:13B97 5AD5F 1EA6E 0
wait 3
log CHECK absent failed to retrieve (spell|effect)
log CHECK logged Combat: actor FF[0-9A-F]+ now also fights player \d+ \(copy [0-9A-F]+\), which attacked it
log CHECK placed fights copy

log DO papyrus Actor StopCombat placed
wait 2
log DO papyrus ObjectReference Disable placed b:false
log DO combat off
wait 2
log CHECK game alive
log DO god off
wait 2
log done
stop
