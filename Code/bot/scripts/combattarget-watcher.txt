# The watching half of the combat target test: is each fight of the other bot's NPCs told here, at its epoch, and only those?
#
#   powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File Tools\VR\run-bot-tests.ps1 -Pair combattarget
#
# The actor runs a bear (A, 23A8A) and Anoriath (B, 13B97) and says, 3 s apart: A fights B, A fights this bot, A fights
# nobody, then A fights B at a stale epoch, which the server must drop.
log combattarget watcher
waitfor connected 45
collect spawn ownership
record combattarget watcher

waitfor known other 40
waitfor known base:23A8A 40
waitfor known base:13B97 40

log A fights B, at the epoch the actor runs it at
waitfor combattarget base:23A8A base:13B97 30
expect combattarget base:23A8A base:13B97

log A fights this bot's character
waitfor combattarget base:23A8A me 10
expect combattarget base:23A8A me

log A fights nobody
waitfor combattarget base:23A8A none 10
expect combattarget base:23A8A none

log the stale word that follows is not passed on
wait 6
expect combattarget base:23A8A none
expect combatwords == 3

log this bot says A fights B, though it does not run A: the server drops it, and the actor hears nothing
npcfight base:23A8A base:13B97
wait 4
expect combatwords == 3
expect connected

record off
report bot-combattarget-watcher.txt
log done
stop
