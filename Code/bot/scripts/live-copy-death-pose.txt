# A copy's body must come to rest where the original's did, and lie as it does, after a death in a fight.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-copy-death-pose
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py --expect-fail live-copy-death-pose     on a build without the fix: must be RED
#   set LIVE_CHECK_DEVBENCH=http://127.0.0.1:8922 first for the flat rig (Tools\Flat\flat-rig.ps1 up)
#
# As live-death-replay-blast: the original, a mage this game runs, dies by the player's Fire Storm (7A82B), which throws
# its ragdoll; its copy gets the recording and the death but no blast, so it falls where it stands.
# Each game drops a body with its own ragdoll. On flat nothing ever moved the copy's ragdoll to where the owner's body
# lies, and on neither build was the copy laid out in the owner's pose (it kept its own fallen pose, moved by one offset).
# The fix: the copy's ragdoll is moved to where the owner's corpse lies, out of view, on flat too, and once the owner's
# bones stop coming it settles once into the owner's last pose.
#
# A run counts only when (REQUIRE): the recording holds the death and the owner sent the body's bones (without them the
# pose part means nothing), the copy stood before it died, and the player saw it when the replay began.
# It is then judged on: nothing moved in view, the copy came to rest within 64 units of where the original did (from
# where each stood), it lies laid out as the original does, the client says its ragdoll now lies near the owner's corpse,
# and it lies still.
#
# Calibration, before any fix code: three runs on 826b0f3e. If any run's pose mean is below 20, the blast did not make
# the poses differ and "pose" proves nothing; the original then dies by two Fire Storms. Two runs of live-death-replay
# give the noise floor; the 15-unit limit stays only if that floor is under 10.
log live copy death pose
waitfor connected 45
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 5
log DO god on

log -- the original: a mage this game runs, 150 units ahead, low on health
log DO place 753CB
wait 8
log DO papyrus Actor SetRestrained placed b:true
log DO papyrus Actor ForceActorValue placed s:Health f:10
wait 3

log -- a Fire Storm kills it while this game samples it and the bot records what this game sends of it
log DO record original placed 16
log DO sleep 2
log DO papyrus Spell Cast 7A82B 14 14
capture newest 12
log REQUIRE bot logged info\] Captured the death of
log REQUIRE bot logged Captured \d+ updates of \w+: .*\([1-9]\d* with bones
log SHOT original of placed

log -- the copy: a mage of the bot's own plays the recording, and this game shows it as a copy
npc captured
waitfor owned == 1 20
wait 8
log REQUIRE npc standing
wait 4
# The client poses no body out of view, and the bot's mage stands 200 units east of the player, often behind the headset.
# Held through the replay and up to the check that nothing moved in view.
log DO look at npc 500 hold 18000
wait 1
log REQUIRE view npc
log DO logmark
log DO record copy npc 33
replay npc
wait 6
# In view, nothing jumps: the ragdoll waits until the player looks away.
log CHECK absent CorpseDiag: \w+'s ragdoll lay \d+ units from where its owner's corpse is; moved there

log -- the player looks away, so the copy's ragdoll can be put where its owner's corpse lies, then looks back
log DO look away npc 500 hold 8000
wait 9
log DO look at npc 500 hold 8000
wait 6
log SHOT copy of npc
log CHECK npc dead
log CHECK place copy like original 64
log CHECK pose copy like original 15
log CHECK logged CorpseDiag: \w+'s ragdoll now lies ([0-9]|[1-5][0-9]) units from where its owner's corpse is
# Its last 4 s, with the player looking at it again: once moved and settled, it lies still.
log CHECK still copy 30 last 4
log CHECK smooth all
wait 4
log CHECK game alive
log DO god off
log done
stop
