# A copy of an NPC thrown to the floor must come to lie where its owner's NPC fell, moved only while the player looks away.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-copy-knockdown-place
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py --expect-fail live-copy-knockdown-place     on a build without the fix: must be RED
#   set LIVE_CHECK_DEVBENCH=http://127.0.0.1:8922 first for the flat rig (Tools\Flat\flat-rig.ps1 up)
#
# The owner's push (PushActorAway, a blast) is not replayed, only its "Ragdoll" action, so the copy collapses where it
# stands while the owner's NPC lands elsewhere. A knocked copy's ragdoll at rest, its owner's still down more than 64 units
# away, is put there once; never in view, so the player must look away for it to happen.
# The recording is live-copy-knockdown's: a Lydia (base A2C8E, made essential) knocked into bleedout at 3 s, pushed over at
# 7 s, stood up at 13 s and set on the player at 15 s. live-copy-knockdown keeps the view on the copy throughout, for its
# slide; this one has three phases while the bot replays:
#   0-9 s, in view: the copy falls and comes to rest; nothing may be moved (the visible phase).
#   9-14 s, looked away, while the owner's NPC still lies: the hidden interval, when the copy may be put where it fell.
#   from 14 s, in view again: the copy gets up and follows; judged after the replay.
# The driver runs the phases with its own sleeps, so all of them are written before the bot's "replay", which goes on meanwhile.
#
# A run counts only when (REQUIRE): the recording holds the owner's fall, the owner's NPC really moved while down, the
# copy is the owner's kind, the player saw the copy as the replay began and did not see it in the hidden interval.
# Pass rules:
#   - visible phase: no "KnockDown: ... its ragdoll was put there" and no "CorpseDiag: ... moved there" line;
#   - the client says the copy's ragdoll was put where its owner's NPC fell;
#   - "down copy like original 64": it lay down within 64 units of where the original did, from where each stood;
#   - "up copy like original 3000": it got up about as long after lying down as the original;
#   - on its feet at the end, and the game alive.
# RED on 826b0f3e (flat and VR): no "put there" line, and "down" fails (the copy collapsed where it stood).
# GREEN: every check ok, three runs of three, on each rig.
log live copy knockdown place
waitfor connected 45
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 5

log DO god on
log -- a Lydia ahead of the player, essential so she goes down instead of dying (the bot's below shares the base)
log DO papyrus ActorBase SetEssential A2C8E b:true
log DO place A2C8E essential
wait 8

log -- the game knocks her down, throws her over, stands her up and sets her on the player while the bot records it
log DO record original placed 28
log DO sleep 3
log DO papyrus Actor DamageActorValue placed s:Health f:100000
log DO sleep 4
log DO papyrus ObjectReference PushActorAway 14 placed f:8
log DO sleep 6
log DO papyrus Actor RestoreActorValue placed s:Health f:100000
log DO combat on
log DO sleep 2
log DO papyrus Actor StartCombat placed 14
capture newest 28
log DO combat off
log REQUIRE bot logged Captured action names of \w+: .*\bRagdoll\b
log REQUIRE logged DownedTravel: own NPC \w+ moved \d+ units in \d+ ms while down

log -- the bot brings its own Lydia and replays the recording on her; the game shows a copy of her
npc temp A2C8E
waitfor owned == 1 20
wait 6
npclife alive 0
wait 3
log -- the visible phase: the player looks at the copy while it falls and comes to rest
log DO look at npc 500 hold 8500
wait 1
log REQUIRE view npc
log DO record copy npc 34
log DO logmark
log DO sleep 8
log CHECK absent KnockDown: NPC copy \w+ lay \d+ units from where its owner's NPC fell
log CHECK absent CorpseDiag: \w+'s ragdoll lay \d+ units from where its owner's corpse is; moved there
log -- the hidden interval: the player looks away while the owner's NPC still lies
log DO look away npc 500 hold 4500
log DO sleep 1
log REQUIRE view npc away
log DO sleep 4
log -- in view again: the copy gets up and follows
log DO look at npc 500 hold 20000
replay npc
wait 3
log SHOT after of npc
log REQUIRE absent Base form differs
log CHECK logged KnockDown: NPC copy \w+ lay \d+ units from where its owner's NPC fell; its ragdoll was put there
log CHECK down copy like original 64
log CHECK up copy like original 3000
log CHECK npc standing
log CHECK game alive
log DO papyrus ActorBase SetEssential A2C8E b:false
log DO god off
wait 3
log done
stop
