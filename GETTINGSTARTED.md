## Windows build

Build the xmake target `Vanilla` for the SE and VR launchers, browser helper and server.

## Stock plugin lists

Mixed SE/VR sessions support the five shared Skyrim masters, with VR's own master
on VR. On the supported SE executable, the SE load order may also include Fishing,
Saints & Seducers, Rare Curios, Survival Mode and `_ResourcePack.esl`. These files
may appear only on the SE side of a mixed pair. Forms from a plugin absent on the
other game do not sync. Both players need matching files for any other shared
content, including paid Creation Club content. Two players on the same edition need
matching shared files, including the bundled Creations they enable.
